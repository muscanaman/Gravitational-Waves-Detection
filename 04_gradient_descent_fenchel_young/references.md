# References

1. Bao, H., Sakaue, S., & Takezawa, Y. (2025). *Any-stepsize Gradient Descent for
   Separable Data under Fenchel–Young Losses.* NeurIPS 2025 (Spotlight).
   [arXiv:2502.04889](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04889)

2. Freund, Y., & Schapire, R. E. (1999). *Large Margin Classification Using the
   Perceptron Algorithm.* Machine Learning, 37(3), 277–296.
   [PDF](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~yfreund/papers/LargeMarginsUsingPerceptron.pdf) ·
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1007662407062)

3. Novikoff, A. B. J. (1962). *On Convergence Proofs on Perceptrons.*
   Proceedings of the Symposium on the Mathematical Theory of Automata, Vol. XII,
   Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn, pp. 615–622. (No open-access digitization
   found; proof reproduced in modern notation in [4].)

4. Mohri, M., & Rostamizadeh, A. *Perceptron Mistake Bounds.*
   [PDF](https://cs.nyu.edu/~mohri/pub/pmb.pdf) — reproduces both the Novikoff
   (1962) and Freund & Schapire (1999) proofs in full, modern notation; used as
   the primary source for the hand-derivations in this repository.
